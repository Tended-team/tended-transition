The transition

One community, two brands

Northern California families have trusted ApexCare for in-home care since 1992. That care now continues under Tended, the in-home elder-care platform and holding company. The design job is continuity: keep the warmth, lead with the new identity.

Usage

Do & don't

Do

  • Lead with Tended purple and warm paper on every page.
  • Reserve gold for one accent per view: dot, rule, or CTA.
  • Reassure first: continuity of care before brand talk.
  • Reference ApexCare warmly, in its own Montserrat, when naming the past.

Don't

  • Blend ApexCare blue/teal/green into Tended surfaces.
  • Recolor, stretch, or rebuild the wordmark or sprout.
  • Use gradient text, glass cards, or side-stripe accents.
  • Write with em dashes; use commas, colons, or periods.
